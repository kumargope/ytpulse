# ⚡ StreamPulse - Ultra Animated YouTube Video Downloader

Ek fully animated, modern aur ultra-fast YouTube Video & Audio Downloader website jisme **koi bhi login ya account ki jarurat nahi hai**.

## 🌟 Features:
- 🚀 **1-Click Auto Paste**: "Paste" button dabate hi clipboard se link auto-paste ho kar turant video details load kar leta hai.
- 🎬 **Multiple Resolutions**: 1080p Full HD, 720p HD, 480p, 360p (MP4 with merged audio).
- 🎵 **High Quality MP3**: Crystal clear audio extractor (192-320kbps).
- 🎨 **Ultra Animated UI**:
  - Interactive Canvas Particles background
  - Neon glows & Cyberpunk glassmorphism design
  - Confetti celebration animation on download trigger
  - Live animated download progress bar with speed and ETA
  - Micro-interactions & Toast alerts
- 🔓 **No Login Required**: 100% Free, Unlimited & Private.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive**: Mobile, Tablet aur PC sabhi devices ke liye optimized.
