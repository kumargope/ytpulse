Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

At StreamPulse, we hold user privacy in the highest regard. We have built our service with a "Zero-Personal-Data" philosophy so you can convert and download media without any tracking or registration.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Because StreamPulse requires No Login, No Account, and No Signup:

  • We do not collect names, passwords, phone numbers, or credit card information.
  • We do not permanently store or archive converted media files on our servers.
  • We do not correlate video URLs with personal user identities.

2. Temporary Processing of Files

When you submit a YouTube URL, our server fetches the stream details and processes the requested format (such as 4K video or 320kbps MP3 audio). Temporary file fragments generated during FFmpeg conversion are purged immediately upon delivery.

3. Local Storage

Your "Recent Downloads" history is stored exclusively in your own web browser's localStorage. It is never transmitted to our backend database and you can clear it anytime with a single click.

4. Third-Party Services

StreamPulse does not sell, rent, or trade your usage information with any third-party marketing companies.
